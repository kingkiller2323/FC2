# Track 2 pre-registration — salience scaling of filming propensity

Committed BEFORE any salience research is run (Ruling H, critical clause).

## The prediction

The model's FITTED baseline filmingPropensity is 0.06 (mundane-to-moderate
salience). Canon's Carrer texture (~4 independent videos from ~36 present, 18
carriers) implies ~11% per-bystander capture ⇒ ~22% per-carrier propensity.

**Pre-registered prediction: at Carrer-grade stakes (a man pinned under a van
— life-threatening, dramatic, sustained), the salience multiplier on baseline
propensity needs to be roughly 0.3–0.5 ABSOLUTE propensity (i.e., a ~5–8×
multiplier over the 0.06 baseline) for the model to reproduce canon's ~11%
per-bystander capture.**

## The test

Research the salience axis independently (bystander videography, smartphone
recording of emergencies and police encounters, crowd behavior at accidents,
the documented record-rather-than-intervene shift). The researcher is BLIND to
this prediction — the research brief must not contain the 0.3–0.5 figure.

- Independently-derived salience scaling lands in 0.3–0.5 absolute (≈5–8×
  baseline) → genuine out-of-sample hit; strongest validation result to date.
- Lands at ~0.05 or ~2.0× → the model or canon is wrong; equally informative.
- No defensible curve exists in the literature → Track 3: promote
  filmingPropensity to a third named design lever (openly a difficulty knob,
  not physics); ledger promotion unblocks on the relabel.

This figure is not consulted again until the research value is fixed.
